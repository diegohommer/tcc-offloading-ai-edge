harder half. Queries are SHUFFLED WITHIN each regime, so the stream is
stationary inside a regime and shifts once, in the middle -- rather

TEST 4 -- when does skipping pay? (the criterion, on two real ladders)
==============================================================================
Skipping to d is cheaper than stepwise iff  E_d < E_onu + b*E_fog + b^2*E_cloud

    beta=0.3: budget    1.115 J  ->  skipping pays for no destination
    beta=0.5: budget    1.821 J  ->  skipping pays for ['fog']

generative/PON tables (J/query @200 tok, layer_energy.yaml)
    ladder [14.8, 196.0, 75.7, 18.76]  monotonic=False
    beta=0.1: budget  203.758 J  ->  skipping pays for ['fog', 'cloud']
    beta=0.3: budget  220.398 J  ->  skipping pays for ['fog', 'cloud']
    beta=0.5: budget  238.540 J  ->  skipping pays for ['fog', 'cloud']

Reading: a monotonic ladder leaves nothing worth skipping toward -- the
cheapest tier above you IS the next one. Skipping pays only where the
ladder inverts, which is what the fog/cloud batching numbers do.
